## 英语十二时态总表

| 时间            | 动作状态                       | 基本结构                       | 核心含义                                                       |
| --------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Present（现在） | Simple（一般）                 | 主语 + 动词原形 / 第三人称单数 | 经常发生的动作、习惯、客观事实                                 |
| Present（现在） | Continuous（进行）             | 主语 + am/is/are + doing       | 此刻或现阶段正在进行的动作                                     |
| Present（现在） | Perfect（完成）                | 主语 + have/has + done         | 过去发生的动作与现在有关，或从过去持续到现在                   |
| Present（现在） | Perfect Continuous（完成进行） | 主语 + have/has been + doing   | 从过去开始持续到现在，或刚刚结束但对现在有影响的动作           |
| Past（过去）    | Simple（一般）                 | 主语 + 动词过去式              | 过去发生的动作或状态                                           |
| Past（过去）    | Continuous（进行）             | 主语 + was/were + doing        | 过去某个时间正在进行的动作                                     |
| Past（过去）    | Perfect（完成）                | 主语 + had + done              | 在过去某个时间点之前，已经发生的动作或已经存在的状态           |
| Past（过去）    | Perfect Continuous（完成进行） | 主语 + had been + doing        | 动作在过去某个时间点之前已经持续了一段时间，强调过程和持续时间 |
| Future（将来）  | Simple（一般）                 | 主语 + will + do               | 将来发生的动作或状态                                           |
| Future（将来）  | Continuous（进行）             | 主语 + will be + doing         | 将来某个时间正在进行的动作                                     |
| Future（将来）  | Perfect（完成）                | 主语 + will have + done        | 将来某个时间点之前已经完成的动作                               |
| Future（将来）  | Perfect Continuous（完成进行） | 主语 + will have been + doing  | 到将来某个时间点，动作将已经持续了一段时间，强调过程和持续时间 |

#### Present Simple（一般现在时）

**核心结构：** `do / does`（实义动词） / `am / is / are`（be 动词）

**核心含义：** 表示经常发生的动作、客观事实、普遍真理或当前状态。

- Facts, truths and generalizations

  客观事实、普遍真理和一般性观点

  ```text
  The sun is bright.
  Pigs don't fly.
  ```

- Habitual actions

  习惯性、经常发生的动作

  ```text
  I eat lunch at noon.
  Seth starts work at 9 am.
  ```

- States and feelings

  表示状态、感受或拥有等

  ```text
  I love my mom.
  It smells good.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  I don't like coffee.
  She doesn't play tennis.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Do you like coffee?
  Does she play tennis?
  ```

#### Past Perfect（过去完成时）

**核心结构：** `had + done`

**核心含义：** 描述过去某个时间点之前已经发生或存在的动作、状态，强调过去的时间先后关系。

- Actions completed before a point in the past

  在过去某个时间点之前，动作已经发生或完成。

  ```text
  I had visited China before I moved there.
  The plane had left by the time I got to the airport.
  ```

- States that existed before a point in the past

  描述在过去某个时间点之前已经存在的状态。

  ```text
  I had owned my computer for 2 months before it broke.
  We had been friends for many years before we got married.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  I had never eaten at that restaurant before I went there yesterday.
  She hadn't been to the circus before she went last week.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Had she eaten lunch by noon?
  Had it rained before they left?

  What had he eaten before lunch?
  How long had she known him before she dated him?
  ```

> - I visited China before I moved there.
> - I had visited China before I moved there.
>
> 一般过去时和过去完成时强调的侧重点不同：
>
> - 一般过去时：按时间顺序叙述过去发生的事情。
> - 过去完成时：站在过去某个时间点，强调更早之前已经发生的事情。

#### Past Continuous（过去进行时）

**核心结构：** `(was / were) + doing`

**核心含义：** 描述过去某个时间点或时间段正在进行的动作，强调动作过程。

- An action ongoing in the past

  过去某个时间点或时间段正在进行的动作。

  ```text
  I was watching TV at 9 pm last night.
  The children were playing outside yesterday afternoon.
  ```

- An ongoing action when another action occurred

  一个动作正在进行时，另一个动作发生了。

  ```text
  I was sleeping when the phone rang.
  We were having dinner when he arrived.
  ```

- Two actions happening at the same time in the past

  两个动作在过去同时进行。

  ```text
  While I was cooking, she was watching TV.
  While he was driving, I was reading.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  I wasn't sleeping when you called.
  She wasn't watching TV at 9 pm.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Were you sleeping when I called?
  What were you doing at 9 pm last night?
  ```

> - She didn't read last night.
> - She was not reading last night.
>
> 一般过去时和过去进行时强调的侧重点不同：
>
> - 一般过去时：关注过去发生了什么。
> - 过去进行时：关注过去某个时间正在做什么。

#### Past Perfect Continuous（过去完成进行时）

**核心结构：** `had been + doing`

**核心含义：** 描述一个动作在过去某个时间点之前已经持续了一段时间，强调动作的过程和持续时间。

- An ongoing action continuing to another point in the past

  一个动作从过去某个时间开始，一直持续到过去的另一个时间点。

  ```text
  I had been waiting for the bus for 2 hours before it arrived.
  He had been driving all day when he arrived.
  ```

- An ongoing action causing a result in the past

  过去持续进行的动作，导致了过去某个时间点出现的结果。

  ```text
  The pavement was wet because it had been raining.
  The children had been playing, so the room was a mess!
  ```

  because / so 负责连接原因和结果，不决定时态。

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  She hadn't been studying for long when she fell asleep.
  It hadn't been snowing for long when it stopped.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Had he been driving all day before he arrived?
  Had the dog been barking because it was scared?

  Why had you been studying so much?
  How long had you been waiting before the bus arrived?
  ```

> - I had studied for two hours when he arrived.
> - I had been studying for two hours when he arrived.
>
> 过去完成时和过去完成进行时强调的侧重点不同：
>
> - 过去完成时：强调过去某个时间点之前已经发生或完成的动作。
> - 过去完成进行时：强调动作在过去某个时间点之前已经持续了一段时间，突出过程和持续时间。

#### Future Simple（一般将来时）

**核心结构：** `will + do` / `(am / is / are) going to + do`

**核心含义：** 描述将来发生的动作或状态。

- Expressing future actions（表达将来的动作或状态）

  ```text
  I will call you tomorrow.
  She will be 18 next year.
  ```

- Expressing future plans and predictions（表达将来的计划和预测）

  ```text
  I'm going to buy a car.
  Look at the clouds! It's going to rain.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  I won't go out tonight.
  She isn't going to come.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Will he come tomorrow?
  Is she going to study tonight?

  When will you leave?
  What are you going to do?
  ```

> - `will`：常用于临时决定、预测或表达意愿。
> - `be going to`：常用于事先计划好的事情，或根据当前迹象作出的预测。

#### Future Perfect（将来完成时）

**核心结构：** `will have + done`

**核心含义：** 描述将来某个时间点之前已经完成的动作，强调动作的完成。

- An action completed before a point in the future

  在将来某个时间点之前，动作已经完成。

  ```text
  I will have finished by 5 pm.
  She will have graduated by June.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  I won't have finished by noon.
  She won't have arrived by then.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Will you have finished by 5 pm?
  Will she have arrived by noon?

  What will you have done by then?
  When will they have finished?
  ```

> - I will finish the work tomorrow.
> - I will have finished the work by tomorrow.
>
> 一般将来时和将来完成时强调的侧重点不同：
>
> - 一般将来时：关注将来会发生什么。
> - 将来完成时：强调到将来某个时间点之前，动作已经完成。

#### Future Continuous（将来进行时）

**核心结构：** `will be + doing` / `(am / is / are) going to be + doing`

**核心含义：** 描述将来某个时间点或时间段正在进行的动作，强调动作过程。

- An action ongoing in the future

  将来某个时间点或时间段正在进行的动作。

  ```text
  I will be working at 9 am.
  She is going to be sleeping tonight.
  ```

- An ongoing action when another action occurs

  一个动作正在进行时，另一个动作将在将来发生。

  ```text
  I will be sleeping when you arrive.
  She is going to be cooking when he gets home.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  I won't be working tomorrow.
  She isn't going to be studying tonight.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Will you be working tomorrow?
  Are they going to be sleeping?

  What will you be doing?
  Where is she going to be staying?
  ```

> - `will be doing`：常用于预测或表达对未来情况的预期。
> - `be going to be doing`：常用于表达已有计划或根据当前迹象作出的预测。

> - I will work tomorrow.
> - I will be working tomorrow.
>
> 一般将来时和将来进行时强调的侧重点不同：
>
> - 一般将来时：关注将来会发生什么。
> - 将来进行时：关注将来某个时间正在进行什么。

#### Future Perfect Continuous（将来完成进行时）

**核心结构：** `will have been + doing`

**核心含义：** 表示动作持续到将来某个时间点，强调持续时间或过程。

- An ongoing action continuing up to a point in the future

  持续到将来某个时间点的动作

  ```text
  I will have been working for 5 hours.
  She will have been sleeping for 4 hours.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  I won't have been working.
  She won't have been sleeping.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Will you have been working?
  How long will she have been sleeping?
  ```

#### Present Continuous（现在进行时）

**核心结构：** `am / is / are + doing`

**核心含义：** 描述现在某个时间点正在进行的动作，或现阶段正在发生的事情，强调动作的过程。

- An action happening right now

  此刻正在发生的动作。

  ```text
  I am studying English now.
  She is cooking dinner.
  They are playing football.
  ```

- Temporary situations happening around now

  现阶段正在发生的事情，不一定是说话的这一刻。

  ```text
  I am learning Japanese these days.
  She is working on a new project.
  We are staying at a hotel this week.
  ```

- Changing and developing situations

  表示正在发生变化或发展的情况。

  ```text
  The weather is getting colder.
  My English is improving.
  The population is increasing.
  ```

- Future arrangements（表示已经安排好的将来计划）

  ```text
  I am meeting him tomorrow.
  She is leaving next Monday.
  We are having dinner tonight.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  I am not sleeping.
  She isn't working today.
  They aren't watching TV.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Are you studying English?
  Is she cooking dinner?

  What are you doing?
  Where are they going?
  ```

> - I read books every day.
> - I am reading a book now.
>
> 一般现在时和现在进行时强调的侧重点不同：
>
> - 一般现在时：关注习惯性、经常发生的动作或客观事实。
> - 现在进行时：关注现在或现阶段正在进行的动作。

#### Present Perfect（现在完成时）

**核心结构：** `have / has + done`

**核心含义：** 描述过去发生的动作或存在的状态，与现在有联系。强调动作的完成、经历，或从过去持续到现在的状态。

- Actions completed with a present result

  过去完成的动作，对现在产生了影响或结果。

  ```text
  I have lost my keys.
  She has finished her homework.
  He has broken his phone.
  ```

- Life experiences（描述人生经历）

  描述截至现在为止曾经经历过的事情，不强调具体发生时间。

  ```text
  I have visited Japan.
  She has never eaten sushi.
  Have you ever been to China?
  ```

- Actions continuing from the past until now

  描述从过去开始，一直持续到现在的状态或动作。

  ```text
  I have lived here for 5 years.
  She has known him since 2020.
  We have been friends for a long time.
  ```

- Actions repeated up to now

  描述从过去到现在重复发生的动作。

  ```text
  I have watched this movie three times.
  She has visited Japan several times.
  We have met many times.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  I haven't finished my homework.
  She hasn't visited Japan.
  They haven't seen this movie.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Have you finished your work?
  Has she ever been to Japan?

  What have you done?
  How long have you lived here?
  ```

> - I lost my keys yesterday.
> - I have lost my keys.
>
> 一般过去时和现在完成时强调的侧重点不同：
>
> - 一般过去时：关注过去某个时间发生的事情，通常会提到明确的过去时间。
> - 现在完成时：关注过去的动作与现在的联系，不强调具体的过去发生时间。
>
> **注意：** 现在完成时通常不与 yesterday、last year、two days ago 等明确的过去时间状语连用。

#### Present Perfect Continuous（现在完成进行时）

**核心结构：** `have / has been + doing`

**核心含义：** 描述一个动作从过去某个时间开始，一直持续到现在，或者刚刚结束但对现在产生影响。强调动作的过程和持续时间。

- An action continuing from the past until now

  一个动作从过去开始，一直持续到现在。

  ```text
  I have been studying English for 3 hours.
  She has been working here since 2020.
  They have been waiting for the bus for 30 minutes.
  ```

- An action that has just stopped with a present result

  一个动作刚刚结束，但其结果或影响仍然体现在现在。

  ```text
  I am tired because I have been running.
  She is wet because she has been walking in the rain.
  His eyes are red because he has been crying.
  ```

- Repeated actions continuing up to now

  描述一段时间内反复发生的动作，强调持续性。

  ```text
  I have been practicing English recently.
  She has been calling me all morning.
  They have been playing tennis every weekend.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  I haven't been sleeping well recently.
  She hasn't been studying for long.
  They haven't been working here very long.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Have you been studying English?
  Has she been working all day?

  What have you been doing?
  How long have you been waiting?
  ```

> - I have studied English for 3 hours.
> - I have been studying English for 3 hours.
>
> 现在完成时和现在完成进行时强调的侧重点不同：
>
> - 现在完成时：强调动作的完成、结果、经历，或持续到现在的状态。
> - 现在完成进行时：强调动作从过去持续到现在的过程和持续时间。
>
> **注意：** 现在完成进行时通常不用于 know、love、believe、own 等表示状态的动词，描述这类持续状态时通常使用现在完成时。

#### Past Simple（一般过去时）

**核心结构：** `动词过去式（did）` / `was / were`（be 动词）

**核心含义：** 描述过去某个时间发生的动作或存在的状态，强调事情发生在过去，与现在没有必然联系。

- Completed actions in the past

  过去已经发生并结束的动作。

  ```text
  I watched a movie yesterday.
  She bought a new computer last week.
  They visited Japan last year.
  ```

- Actions that happened in sequence

  描述过去按照时间顺序发生的一系列动作。

  ```text
  I woke up, brushed my teeth, and went to work.
  She opened the door and walked into the room.
  He got up, had breakfast, and left home.
  ```

- Past habits and repeated actions

  描述过去经常发生的习惯性动作。

  ```text
  I played football when I was young.
  She walked to school every day.
  We often visited our grandparents.
  ```

- States in the past

  描述过去存在的状态或情况。

  ```text
  I was very shy when I was a child.
  She lived in Beijing in 2020.
  They were happy yesterday.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  I didn't watch TV yesterday.
  She didn't go to school.
  They weren't at home last night.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Did you watch the movie?
  Did she go to work yesterday?
  Were they happy?

  What did you do yesterday?
  Where did she go last night?
  ```

> - I watched TV last night.
> - I was watching TV at 9 pm last night.
>
> 一般过去时和过去进行时强调的侧重点不同：
>
> - 一般过去时：关注过去发生了什么，强调动作或事件本身。
> - 过去进行时：关注过去某个时间正在做什么，强调动作过程。

#### Past Future Simple（过去将来一般时）

**核心结构：** `would + do` / `was / were going to + do`

**核心含义：** 从过去某个时间点出发，描述当时认为将来会发生的动作或状态。

- Actions expected to happen in the future from a past perspective

  从过去的角度描述将来会发生的事情。

  ```text
  He said he would come tomorrow.
  I knew she would succeed.
  She promised she would help me.
  ```

- Past plans and intentions

  描述过去已经有的计划或打算。

  ```text
  I was going to buy a car.
  She was going to study abroad.
  They were going to move to Japan.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  I wouldn't go there.
  She wasn't going to come.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Would he come?
  Was she going to study abroad?

  What would you do?
  ```

> - He says he will come tomorrow.
> - He said he would come the next day.
>
> 一般将来时和过去将来时强调的侧重点不同：
>
> - 一般将来时：站在现在看将来。
> - 过去将来时：站在过去看将来。

#### Past Future Continuous（过去将来进行时）

**核心结构：** `would be + doing`

**核心含义：** 从过去某个时间点出发，描述当时预计在未来某个时间正在进行的动作。

- An action expected to be ongoing in the future from a past perspective

  从过去的角度描述将来某个时间正在进行的动作。

  ```text
  He said he would be working at 9 am.
  She thought I would be sleeping.
  I knew they would be waiting for us.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  He wouldn't be working tomorrow.
  She wouldn't be waiting for us.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Would you be working at 9 am?
  Would she be sleeping when we arrived?
  ```

> - He said he would work tomorrow.
> - He said he would be working tomorrow.
>
> 过去将来一般时和过去将来进行时强调的侧重点不同：
>
> - 过去将来一般时：关注从过去视角看，未来会发生什么。
> - 过去将来进行时：关注从过去视角看，未来某个时间正在进行什么。

#### Past Future Perfect（过去将来完成时）

**核心结构：** `would have + done`

**核心含义：** 从过去某个时间点出发，描述预计在过去视角下的将来某个时间点之前已经完成的动作。

- An action expected to be completed before a future point from a past perspective

  从过去的角度描述未来某个时间点之前将会完成的动作。

  ```text
  He thought he would have finished the work by Friday.
  She said she would have graduated by June.
  I knew they would have arrived by then.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  He wouldn't have finished by noon.
  She wouldn't have arrived by then.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Would you have finished by Friday?
  Would she have graduated by June?
  ```

> - He thinks he will have finished by Friday.
> - He thought he would have finished by Friday.
>
> 将来完成时和过去将来完成时强调的侧重点不同：
>
> - 将来完成时：站在现在看将来，强调到将来某个时间点之前已经完成。
> - 过去将来完成时：站在过去看将来，强调到过去视角下的未来某个时间点之前已经完成。

#### Past Future Perfect Continuous（过去将来完成进行时）

**核心结构：** `would have been + doing`

**核心含义：** 从过去某个时间点出发，描述预计到未来某个时间点，某个动作将已经持续了一段时间，强调动作的过程和持续时间。

- An action expected to have continued for a period of time by a future point from a past perspective

  从过去的角度描述，到未来某个时间点，动作将已经持续了一段时间。

  ```text
  He thought that by June, he would have been working there for 5 years.
  She knew that by midnight, she would have been studying for 4 hours.
  I expected that by next month, I would have been living here for a year.
  ```

- Negative sentences（否定句）

  ```text
  He wouldn't have been working there for 5 years by June.
  She wouldn't have been studying for 4 hours by midnight.
  ```

- Questions（疑问句）

  ```text
  Would he have been working there for 5 years by June?
  How long would she have been studying by midnight?
  ```

> - He thinks that by June, he will have been working there for 5 years.
> - He thought that by June, he would have been working there for 5 years.
>
> 将来完成进行时和过去将来完成进行时强调的侧重点不同：
>
> - 将来完成进行时：站在现在看将来，强调到将来某个时间点动作已经持续了多久。
> - 过去将来完成进行时：站在过去看将来，强调到过去视角下的未来某个时间点动作将已经持续了多久。

#### 英语时态体系总结

| 时间视角                | 时态形式                                           |   数量 |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -----: |
| Present（现在）         | Simple / Continuous / Perfect / Perfect Continuous |      4 |
| Past（过去）            | Simple / Continuous / Perfect / Perfect Continuous |      4 |
| Future（将来）          | Simple / Continuous / Perfect / Perfect Continuous |      4 |
| Past Future（过去将来） | Simple / Continuous / Perfect / Perfect Continuous |      4 |
| **合计**                |                                                    | **16** |

**补充说明：**

严格来说，英语传统教学中的「16 种时态」是一种便于学习的分类方式。

现代语言学通常将时态（Tense）和体（Aspect）区分开来。过去将来时更多被视为过去视角下的将来表达，而不是独立的语法时态。

建议将过去将来时作为十二时态之后的扩展章节学习。
