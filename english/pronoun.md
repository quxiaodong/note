| 主体 | be  | 缩写    | possessive adjective | possessive pronoun |
| ---- | --- | ------- | -------------------- | ------------------ |
| I    | am  | I'm     | My                   | Mine               |
| You  | are | You're  | Your                 | Yours              |
| He   | is  | He's    | His                  | His                |
| She  | is  | She's   | Her                  | Hers               |
| It   | is  | It's    | Its                  | -                  |
| We   | are | We're   | Our                  | Ours               |
| They | are | They're | Their                | Theirs             |

```
I am a student.
Am I a student?

Yes, I am.
No, I'm not.
No, I am not.
```

```
You are a student.
Are you a student?

Yes, I am.
No, I'm not.
No, I am not.
```

```
He is a student.
Is he a student?

Yes, he is.
No, he isn't.
No, he's not.
No, he is not.
```

```
She is a student.
Is she a student?

Yes, she is.
No, she isn't.
No, she's not.
No, she is not.
```

```
It is a dog.
Is it a dog?

Yes, it is.
No, it isn't.
No, it's not.
No, it is not.

This is its ball. // its 通常需要修饰一个名词，不能像 mine、yours 那样直接独立使用
```
